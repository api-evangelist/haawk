# HAAWK

> HAAWK is a Los Angeles-based music rights management and monetization company that administers digital rights across YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok for record labels, music publishers, and independent artists. HAAWK has administered 100B+ video views and manages 1.1M+ music copyrights for 2,000+ rights holders. Backed by 500 Global.

Note: As of 2026-07-19 enrichment, HAAWK publishes no public developer API, SDK, or technical documentation. This profile covers the company's public product and policy surface only.

## Products

- [HAAWK](https://www.haawk.com/products): Full-service YouTube Content ID and Facebook/Instagram (Meta) Rights Manager administration for labels, publishers, and music libraries.
- [Identifyy](https://www.haawk.com/products): Self-service rights management platform for independent artists to monetize user-generated content videos.
- [Keyframe Audio](https://www.haawk.com/products): Micro sync-licensing catalog of 50,000+ production music tracks (from $19/month).
- [Diistro](https://www.haawk.com/products): Digital music distribution to Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube Music, TikTok, and others.
