SESAC acquires HAAWK, Inc. HAAWK Lands at SESAC Music Group (New York, NY — October 22, 2024) — SESAC Music Group announced today its acquisition of HAAWK , a media software and services company specializing in copyright management and monetization for independent music, film, television, and video catalogs. HAAWK (Helping All Assets With Knowledge) provides full-service administration of YouTube’s Content ID system and Facebook’s Rights Manager platforms (including Instagram), serving a broad range of record labels, publishers, and music rightsholder clients.
