HAAWK Lands at #433 on the 2023 Deloitte Fast 500 HAAWK Ranked Number 100 Fastest-Growing Company in North America on the 2023 Deloitte Technology Fast 500™ Attributes 275% Revenue Growth to the continued proliferation of user-generated video content Los Angeles, CA, November 9, 2023 — HAAWK, Inc. (“ HAAWK ”) today announced it ranked number 433 on the Deloitte Technology Fast 500 ™, a ranking of the 500 fastest-growing technology, media, telecommunications, life sciences, fintech, and energy tech companies in North America, now in its 29th year. HAAWK grew 275% during this period.
