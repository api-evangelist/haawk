HAAWK Lands at #23 in Media on the 2023 Inc 5000 List of the Fastest-Growing Companies in America Attributes 275% Revenue Growth to the continued proliferation of user-generated video content Los Angeles, CA, August 15, 2023 — HAAWK, Inc. (“ HAAWK ”) today announced it has been ranked as #23 in the media industry, #126 in the Los Angeles area, #291 in California, and #2,039 overall on the 2023 Inc. 5000 , a ranking of the 5000 fastest-growing companies in the United States of America.
