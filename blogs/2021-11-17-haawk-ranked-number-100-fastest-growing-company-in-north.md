HAAWK Ranked Number 100 Fastest-Growing Company in North America on the 2021 Deloitte Technology Fast 500™ HAAWK, Inc. ranked #100 on Deloitte’s 2021 Technology Fast 500 Los Angeles, CA, November 17, 2021 — HAAWK, Inc. (“ HAAWK ”) today announced it ranked number 100 on the Deloitte Technology Fast 500 ™, a ranking of the 500 fastest-growing technology, media, telecommunications, life sciences, fintech, and energy tech companies in North America, now in its 27th year.
